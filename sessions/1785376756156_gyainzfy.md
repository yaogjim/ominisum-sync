## 正文

Jul 16, 2026

\|

We’re renaming NotebookLM to Gemini Notebook. It's the same standalone product, now doing more across the Google ecosystem and updated with a secure cloud computer.

---

---

\[\[duration\]\] minutes

We introduced NotebookLM at Google I/O 2023 as [Project Tailwind](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/ai/notebooklm-google-ai/) with a simple goal: help people learn. Now, more than 30 million people and over 600,000 organizations are using it to transform how they work, from business owners creating interactive onboarding materials to students converting notes into audio and video summaries.

Today, we’re renaming NotebookLM to Gemini Notebook. It remains a standalone product focused on being your premier research tool, but it will now do more across the Google ecosystem, including inside the Gemini app and Google Search.

#### Explore under-the-hood upgrades

To make your research more accurate and powerful, we’ve started to roll out an [update](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/notebooklm/better-research-notebooklm/) that gives every notebook a secure cloud computer. This allows Gemini Notebook to write and execute code natively, helping you conduct complex data analysis grounded in your sources. This is available today for Google AI Ultra users and Workspace business customers with AI Ultra Access and AI Expanded Access. It will roll out to all Pro users on the web over the coming weeks, enabling entirely new output formats and deeper analysis.

#### Take your notebooks everywhere

Just like a physical notebook, your digital notebooks should go wherever you work. You can already access and create notebooks directly within the Gemini app, with full cross-app syncing between the Gemini app and the standalone Gemini Notebook experience. Soon, we’ll also bring notebooks directly into AI Mode in Search.

To everyone who has been with us since Project Tailwind in 2023, thank you. We’re excited to keep building this ecosystem with you. [Try out](https://notebooklm.google/?gad_source=1&gad_campaignid=22476587015&gbraid=0AAAAA-fwSscqtrhVpcipFVkJMXYsuA8MR&gclid=Cj0KCQjwjb3SBhDgARIsAMKiWzjbaqrAcM9XauQ4xuqVaxDyEmGCoK6g8Bq8FKd-8MpRRLQCIMgPkR8aApMyEALw_wcB) the new features and let us know what you learn.

## 通俗精炼（1）

### 2026-07-30 01:59:01

#### NotebookLM 更名为 Gemini Notebook：从独立研究工具走向 Gemini 生态

##### 核心变化

Google 将 **NotebookLM 正式更名为 Gemini Notebook**。产品仍保持独立，继续定位为基于用户资料的研究与学习工具，但将更深度地融入 **Gemini 应用与 Google 搜索**。

目前，NotebookLM 已拥有超过 **3000 万用户**，服务超过 **60 万家组织**。

##### 能力升级：引入安全云电脑

每个笔记本将逐步配备一台**安全云电脑**，使 Gemini Notebook 能够直接编写并运行代码，从而基于用户提供的资料完成：

- 复杂数据分析
- 更深入的研究与推理
- 更多类型的内容输出

该功能已向 Google AI Ultra 用户及部分 Workspace 商业客户开放，未来几周将逐步覆盖网页版 Pro 用户。

##### 生态整合：笔记跨产品流转

用户已经可以在 Gemini 应用中创建和访问笔记本，并与独立版 Gemini Notebook 实时同步。

下一步，Google 还计划将笔记本能力整合进 Google 搜索的 **AI Mode**，让研究资料能在更多工作场景中持续复用。

##### 产品意义

这次更名不只是品牌调整，而是产品战略升级：

> Gemini Notebook 正从单一的资料整理与总结工具，演变为具备代码执行、数据分析和跨应用协同能力的个人研究工作台。
