## 正文

**Nicolas Finet**

### 如何在 GLM-5.2 上构建 AI GTM 大脑：一个您可自行运行的基于信号的外发系统

I have run

[@Sortlist](https://x.com/@Sortlist) for ten years and watched the same pattern hold the whole time: a company goes looking for help the week something shifts inside it, a hire into a budget, a fresh round, a competitor that just beat them to a deal. Reach them that week and your reply rate sits between 15 and 25 percent. Reach them any other week and you are noise.

接下来正是构建能够捕捉那些周的系统的具体方法，一步步地进行，中间穿插着代码。最后我会给你整个仓库。

---

#### 首先确保订单正确

大多数外发内容都是以发送者为优先构建的。

你购买发送工具，导入名单，开始发送，而时机却成了事后才想到的事。这正是为什么回复会枯竭。

以另一种方式构建它。

Find the company that changed something this week, then write the message as your reaction to that change. Get the order right and you stop needing a bigger list or a rented intent feed. Every step below is that one idea, wired to run on its own.

---

### Step 1. Put GLM-5.2 behind the brain

Start with the reasoning layer, because it is the part everything else leans on. The brain talks to its model through the Anthropic Messages API, and GLM-5.2 speaks that API natively, so it drops in with no code change. You point three environment variables at it and you are done.

```bash
# optional: install Claude Code as the orchestrator (the repo also runs on plain Python)
curl -fsSL https://claude.ai/install.sh | bash

# get a GLM-5.2 key at z.ai/manage-apikey, then point the brain at it (.env or your shell)
ANTHROPIC_BASE_URL=https://api.z.ai/api/anthropic
ANTHROPIC_AUTH_TOKEN=your_zai_key      # bearer token, NOT ANTHROPIC_API_KEY
GTM_BRAIN_MODEL=glm-5.2                 # or glm-5.2[1m] for the 1M-token window
```

What it entails in money: GLM-5.2 runs about $1.40 per million input tokens and $4.40 per million output, which at this system's scale is a few dollars a month. Our full fleet at Sortlist, fourteen agents doing heavy research all day, runs around four hundred dollars a month. If you want zero cost, change one line, GTM\_BRAIN\_MODEL=glm-4.7-flash, and it runs on a free model through the same endpoint, or point the base URL at a local Ollama and nothing leaves your machine.

What good looks like. A one-line test comes back from GLM-5.2, not from Anthropic, and you are billed cents.

Where it breaks. Pasting the key into ANTHROPIC\_API\_KEY instead of ANTHROPIC\_AUTH\_TOKEN.

[Z.ai](http://z.ai/) authenticates with a bearer token, and the wrong variable silently falls back to the offline heuristic, so your drafts come out wooden and you blame the model. It is the single most common setup mistake.

---

### Step 2. Clone the brain and run it dry

Before you wire a single real source, run the whole loop on sample data so you can see it work. It drafts and prints, and it sends nothing.

```bash
git clone https://github.com/nifinet/gtm-brain.git && cd gtm-brain
python3 -m venv .venv && source .venv/bin/activate
pip install -r requirements.txt

python eval.py --offline     # grade the judge against a golden set, no key needed
python run.py --offline      # run the full loop on sample signals, sends NOTHING
```

What good looks like. The gate prints PASS, then the brain ranks a handful of accounts, drafts an opener for the hot ones, and labels every send DRAFT (dry run, nothing queued).

Where it breaks. Reaching for --live on day one. A system that can enroll on its own and drafts one confident, wrong opener will mail your whole list before a human sees it. Everything stays dry until you have read real drafts and passed the gate in step 6.

---

### Step 3. Watch for movement, do not rent a list

The motion starts from something that moved, sorted into five buckets that are not equal in weight:

```javascript
demand   a company is searching, right now, for what you sell
funding  a round or acquisition, fresh budget with a mandate
job      a role opened or reposted, a budget-owning hire started
company  a launch, an expansion, a visible change in their stack
social   they engaged a competitor or posted about the problem
```

The repo ships a real, no-auth adapter that reads public job boards, so "free signals" is a working example, not a claim:

```python
# adapters/signals_stub.py -> swap in your own; this one is real and free
from adapters.signals_stub import GreenhouseSignals
signals = GreenhouseSignals(["stripe", "ramp"], keywords=["growth", "marketing"]).fetch()
# every open role becomes a 'job' signal. point the 'demand' bucket at Sortlist for buyer search.
```

Four of these buckets you can research off the public web for free, and they carry the system well on their own.

第五，需求，是不同的档位：一家公司在还没告诉任何一个销售人员之前，就已经在寻找你到底是做什么的。这种情况在公开网络上很难被捕捉到，而这正是 Sortlist 花了十年时间关注的事情，所以当你需要更多曝光时，可以通过 Sortlist 接入它。这个 repo 在没有它的情况下运行良好，有了它，它只是运行得更远。

The detail most people miss in the free buckets is the reposted role: the same job listed twice usually means the first hire fell through, and that company is in more pain than the one that posted once.

What good looks like. A short ranked list where an account that a fresh round and a live search both point to lands on top, each row carrying a why-now a rep could say out loud.

Where it breaks. Sensing everything and ranking nothing. Cluster and rank at the door, or the sorting problem lands on the send and it gets noisier every day.

---

### Step 4. Let GLM-5.2 decide who gets a message

Only after a signal exists does the brain decide anything, and the deciding is the actual moat. GLM-5.2 reads the trigger, the account's full history, and a plain description of who you want as a customer, then returns a score, a why-now that quotes the real trigger, and one play.

The reasoning lives in a plain file you can edit without touching code:

```javascript
# prompts/judge.md
score 0-100 on timing + fit:
   80-100  strong fit + high intent (a live demand search, funding, or 2 signals clustered)
   50-79   good fit, one solid signal
   20-49   weak fit, or a lone low-intent signal
    0-19   off-ICP, or noise

rules:
   touched in the last 7 days?  -> nurture or skip, never pile on
   weak signal or off-ICP?      -> skip. saying no is the job
   why_now must quote the real trigger, never a generic value prop
```

Saying no is most of the work.

A system that skips three accounts out of four is worth more than one that mails all four, because the no is where the targeting lives.

What good looks like. Hot accounts come back with a why-now you could paste straight into an email. Lukewarm ones come back as skip, with a reason.

Where it breaks. Scoring a signal without the account's history. Strip the memory and GLM-5.2 cannot tell someone you chased twice from a stranger, so it recommends the same opener again and you read like a stalker.

---

### Step 5. Write the opener from the trigger

Now, and only now, does anything get written. The rule is one sentence: the first line quotes the trigger back in the buyer's own words. Never "Hi {{firstName}}". The voice lives in another editable file:

```javascript
# prompts/draft.md
- open on the real trigger, in the buyer's words. never "Hi {{firstName}}".
- tie it to one problem you solve, in a single sentence.
- end with one low-friction ask. no fake urgency, no "circling back", no em dashes.
```

If the message could have gone out last month unchanged, the brain skipped its own job and the draft gets thrown away. A real trigger does not need nine follow-ups to manufacture relevance, so the sequence stays short.

What good looks like. Ten drafts read back to back, all different, because the triggers are all different.

Where it breaks. Padding the sequence back to nine touches to "stay top of mind." Length is what you reach for when the opener is weak.

---

### Step 6. Make it grade itself before it sends

This is the part most "AI SDR" demos quietly skip, and it is the one that earns trust. Before a live send, the brain grades its own judgment against a labelled golden set, and once real outcomes exist it checks whether the accounts it scored hot actually win more than the ones it scored cold.

If it cannot prove that, it does not send.

```bash
python eval.py            # grades the judge vs the golden set, exits nonzero on failure
python run.py --live      # sends for real, and is blocked automatically if the gate fails
```

The learn loop tunes the surface, which copy wins.

This gate grades the foundation, whether the score means anything at all. Almost everyone optimizes the surface on top of a foundation they never checked.

What good looks like. The gate runs offline and free before every send, and a miscalibrated judge gets stopped instead of let loose on your domain.

Where it breaks. Overriding the gate because the pipeline looks thin this week. The one run you skip the check is the one that mails your whole list off a target nobody verified.

---

### Step 7. Close the loop and put it on a schedule

An agent that does not learn is a faster script. Feed every outcome back in, and the buckets that book meetings get heavier while the openers that die get cut.

```python
from brain.memory import Memory
from brain import loop
loop.record_result(Memory("gtm_brain.db"), account="apexnorth.agency", touch_id=42, result="replied")
# replied | meeting | bounced | no_reply  ->  re-weights the buckets and the winning opener
```

Then, because the whole motion is one command, a single cron line runs it before you wake up.

```bash
0 8 * * *  cd ~/gtm-brain && . .venv/bin/activate && python run.py >> run.log 2>&1
```

What good looks like. Month one the brain weights the buckets the way you guessed. Month three it weights them the way your market actually behaves, because it spent two months marking its own homework.

Where it breaks. Logging sends but never logging outcomes. With no reply data the weights never move, and you are paying GLM-5.2 to run an expensive scheduler.

---

### What the morning looks like now

I open my laptop to a short ranked list, each account with one line under it saying why it is there this week. I read it in two minutes and pick: send, edit, or skip. GLM-5.2 built the list and the openers overnight.

在我们自己的销售漏斗中，两周内安排了19场会议，在142条信号验证线索中，回复率为12%。资金信号和第一方拜访促成了大部分已预约的通话。单独的社交点赞是被跳过的部分，而跳过它们正是其余部分得以成功安排的原因。

##### 1. 先把顺序搞对：先找变化，再写外发

传统外发通常是：买工具、导入名单、开始发送，最后才考虑“时机”。作者认为这正是回复率下降的原因。

正确顺序应该是：

- 先找到 **本周发生变化的公司**
- 再把邮件写成对这个变化的回应
- 不需要更大的名单，也不需要租用昂贵的意图数据

作者提到，在 Sortlist 十年经验中，如果在公司发生关键变化的那一周联系他们，回复率可达到 **15% 到 25%**；如果在其他时间联系，大多只是噪音。

常见触发变化包括：

- 新融资
- 新招聘或关键岗位开放
- 竞争对手刚赢走一单
- 公司启动新项目或扩张
- 正在主动搜索相关服务

##### 4. Step 3：监测“变化信号”，而不是租名单

系统从“公司发生了什么变化”开始。文章将信号分成五类，权重并不相同：

| 信号类型 | 含义 |
|---|---|
| demand | 公司正在主动搜索你所卖的东西 |
| funding | 新融资、并购，意味着新预算和新目标 |
| job | 新岗位发布、重新发布，或预算负责人入职 |
| company | 产品发布、扩张、技术栈变化等 |
| social | 与竞争对手互动，或公开谈到相关问题 |

项目仓库内置了一个免费、无需认证的公共招聘信号适配器，例如读取 Greenhouse 招聘信息：

```python
from adapters.signals_stub import GreenhouseSignals

signals = GreenhouseSignals(
    ["stripe", "ramp"],
    keywords=["growth", "marketing"]
).fetch()
```

每个开放岗位都可以成为一个 `job` 信号。

###### 重要细节：重复发布的岗位更有价值

作者特别指出，很多人忽略了“重新发布的岗位”。

如果同一个职位发布了两次，通常说明：

- 第一次招聘失败
- 该岗位需求仍未解决
- 公司痛点可能更强烈

这类公司通常比只发布一次岗位的公司更值得关注。

###### 关于 demand 信号

前四类信号大多可以从公开网络免费获取。但 `demand` 不同，它表示：

> 一家公司在联系销售之前，就已经在主动寻找你提供的服务。

这种信号很难从公开网络捕捉，Sortlist 花了十年追踪这类需求。免费仓库不依赖它也能运行，但接入后系统覆盖范围会更强。

---

##### 5. Step 4：让 GLM-5.2 判断谁值得被联系

只有在出现信号后，系统才开始判断是否值得发消息。

GLM-5.2 会读取：

- 当前触发信号
- 该账户完整历史记录
- 理想客户画像 ICP
- 过去是否联系过
- 是否近期触达过

然后输出：

- 0-100 分评分
- “为什么现在联系”的理由
- 推荐打法
- 是否跳过

判断规则放在普通文本文件中，方便修改：

```javascript
score 0-100 on timing + fit:
80-100 strong fit + high intent
50-79 good fit, one solid signal
20-49 weak fit
0-19 off-ICP, or noise

rules:
touched in the last 7 days? -> nurture or skip
weak signal or off-ICP? -> skip
why_now must quote the real trigger
```

###### 核心观点：会拒绝才是真正的智能

作者强调，系统大部分工作不是“发给谁”，而是“不要发给谁”。

一个能跳过四个账户中三个的系统，往往比把四个都发出去的系统更有价值，因为真正的精准定位体现在拒绝里。

###### 常见问题

如果只看信号、不看账户历史，系统可能不知道这个客户已经被追过两次，于是又推荐相同开场白，显得像骚扰。

---

##### 6. Step 5：根据触发信号写开场白

只有当系统判断该账户值得联系时，才开始写邮件。

最重要规则是：

> 第一句话必须引用真实触发信号，用买家的语言表达。

不要写：

```text
Hi {{firstName}}
```

而是直接从事件切入。

邮件风格规则也放在可编辑文件中：

```javascript
- open on the real trigger, in the buyer's words
- tie it to one problem you solve
- end with one low-friction ask
- no fake urgency
- no "circling back"
```

###### 判断好坏的标准

如果这封邮件上个月也能原样发送，说明它没有真正利用本周信号，应该丢弃。

好的结果应该是：

- 10 封草稿读起来都不同
- 因为每封都围绕不同触发事件写成

作者也反对把序列硬拉长到 9 次触达。真正相关的开场白，不需要靠很多跟进制造存在感。

---

##### 7. Step 6：发送前让系统自我评分

这是很多“AI SDR”演示会跳过的一步，但作者认为这是建立信任的关键。

在真正发送前，系统会用标注好的 golden set 测试自己的判断能力：

```bash
python eval.py
python run.py --live
```

如果判断器没通过评估，`run.py --live` 会被自动阻止。

###### 评估重点

学习循环可以优化“哪种文案更有效”，但评估门槛要检查的是更基础的问题：

> 这个分数到底有没有意义？

也就是说，系统需要验证：

- 被评为高分的账户，是否真的更可能赢单
- 被评为低分的账户，是否确实不值得联系

###### 常见风险

不要因为本周 pipeline 不够，就手动绕过检查。作者警告：最容易出事的就是那次你跳过校验，然后系统把未经验证的目标群体全部发了一遍。

---

##### 8. Step 7：闭环学习，并定时运行

一个不会学习的代理，只是更快的脚本。

系统需要把每次结果反馈回去：

```python
from brain.memory import Memory
from brain import loop

loop.record_result(
    Memory("gtm_brain.db"),
```
