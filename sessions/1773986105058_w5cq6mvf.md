## 正文

**Bo Wang**

Great interview of @StefanoErmon  
by @ziv\_ravid  
in the latest episode of Information Bottleneck — worth a watch: https://x.com/ziv\_ravid/status/2034692961359876550?s=20…

Really resonate with Stefano’s framing: autoregressive models are like typewriters, diffusion models are editors.

When we built scGPT three years ago, we went with autoregressive training—it was the natural choice at the time.

But three years later, I think we got the architecture wrong. Diffusion LMs feel much better aligned with what biological omics actually needs.

There’s also an underappreciated advantage on the reasoning side for diffusion models in biology. More on that soon—stay tuned 👀  
对 @StefanoErmon 的精彩采访 @StefanoErmon  
由 @ziv\_ravid  
在《Information Bottleneck》最新一期中——值得一看： https://x.com/ziv\_ravid/status/2034692961359876550?s=20…

非常共鸣于 Stefano 的表述：自回归模型就像打字机，扩散模型是编辑。

三年前我们开发 scGPT 时，选择了自回归训练——这在当时是很自然的选择。

但三年后，我认为我们的架构搞错了。扩散语言模型感觉更契合生物组学实际所需。

在生物学中，扩散模型还有一个被忽视的推理优势。更多相关内容即将揭晓——敬请关注 👀
