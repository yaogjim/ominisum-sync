**The Startup Ideas Podcast (SIP)**

### What is Andrej Karpathy's autoresearch?

Andrej Karpathy, one of the godfathers of AI, launched an open-source project called autoresearch. It already has 25,000+ GitHub stars and it's growing fast.

![Image](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HDKjPOpawAAan5d?format=jpg&name=large)

Here's the simple version: you give it a goal. An AI agent plans what to do, edits the Python code, runs a short training experiment on a GPU (about five minutes), reads the results, and decides what to change next.

Then it repeats the loop.

You tell the AI what "better" means: cheaper leads, more clicks, higher sales, better model score. The AI keeps changing things, testing them, and only saves the changes that improve.

You wake up. You grab the best version. You turn it into something you charge for (or give away).

Toby Lutke, CEO and co-founder of Shopify, put it this way: autoresearch works for optimizing any piece of software. Make a folder, add a program markdown file, create a bench script, make a branch, and let it rip.

![Image](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HDKj-iKbQAAb1Cy?format=jpg&name=large)

When Karpathy and Toby are both paying attention to the same thing, you pay attention too.

The Mental Model

Think of it like a research boss you can boss around.

![Image](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HDKjdITa4AAac46?format=jpg&name=large)

Step 1: Write a clear task. For code experiments: "improve this model test score." For business: "figure out the top five competitors for product XYZ and make a short report."

Step 2: Give the bot access. Code and a GPU for ML experiments. Internet and documents for reading tasks.

Step 3: The bot runs the loop. It plans. It acts (runs code or searches). It reads results. It updates the plan.

Step 4: Come back later (6, 12, 20 hours). Everything is logged: charts, metrics, and a written summary in plain language.

#### 10 Business Ideas Built on Autoresearch

![Image](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HDKkB8WbQAMoY8V?format=jpg&name=large)

1\. Niche Agent-in-a-Box Products

Package tiny autoresearch loops tuned for one painful niche. An Amazon listing experimenter. An email sequence tuner for realtors. A pricing optimizer for SaaS.

The value prop: this thing runs experiments for you 24/7 and shows you the winner to click "accept." Charge a monthly fee.

2\. A/B Testing for Marketing (on Autopilot)

The agent writes variants of headlines, layouts, and offers. It pushes them to traffic. It measures which one converts better. It keeps iterating.

Think of it as the future of tools like Optimizely. Autoresearch for landing pages. Autoresearch for ad creatives, angles, and audiences. It keeps the combos that lower CAC or raise ROAS.

Run it for your own products. Or sell an always-on experiment engine to clients as a retainer. $5K/month for the best landing pages delivered to their inbox.

3\. Research as a Service

Autoresearch's recipe is a loop for doing research: searching, reading, summarizing, comparing, repeating.

Point that at money problems. Constantly updated competitor reports for startups (pricing, features, gaps). Fast due diligence summaries for investors. Compliance and regulation tracking for crypto, healthcare, or finance.

Charge per report or monthly for always-fresh dashboards.

4\. Power Tool Inside Your Own SaaS

If you already have a SaaS or workflow, embed an autoresearch agent so your users can press one button: Optimize.

The system runs a mini research loop for them. Tune prompts. Pick best pricing. Rank suppliers.

Charge higher tiers for this feature. Or use it as a wedge to upsell Pro and Enterprise plans.

5\. "We Run More Tests Than Anyone" Agency

Autoresearch lets you run hundreds of experiments instead of a few. Simple pitch: we do 100x more testing than other shops for the same or lower fee.

Niche examples: Shopify store conversion lab. B2B SaaS pricing experiments. Email subject line and sequence optimizer.

Charge monthly retainer plus a performance bonus tied to KPI lifts.

6\. Auto Quant for Trading Ideas

Use autoresearch to run small, fast backtests of many simple trading rules. LLM-based factor screens. Sentiment filters. All on one GPU overnight.

Keep the few strategies that look promising. Trade on your own account or sell signals and strategy reports as a digital product.

One caveat from the episode: you need a human in the loop. People who blindly trust autoresearch for trading will get burned.

7\. Always-On Lead Qualification

Point an autoresearch agent at your CRM and inbound leads. Let it test rules and messages to see which leads are most likely to buy.

It auto-grades leads. Suggests next actions. Drafts follow-ups. Salespeople only focus on high-value deals. Revenue per hour goes up.

8\. Finance Ops Autopilot

Use the loop to grind through invoice matching, expense report generation, and exception detection. Continuous small improvements to rules and prompts.

Sell it as: "We cut your AP/expense time in half." Software or managed service. Start as a service, evolve into the software.

This is the kind of product that gets acquired by a large fintech company or bank.

9\. Internal Productivity Lab

Treat your company like Karpathy's GPU lab. Define KPIs: response time, close rate, ticket resolution. Let agents iterate on workflows, templates, and routing rules.

Fewer meetings. Less manual grunt work. You personally touch only the high-impact decisions. Everyone else rides the improved process.

10\. Done-for-You Due Diligence Shop

Use the research loop to chew through docs, filings, product pages, and reviews. Maintain an evolving living memo for clients: investors, acquirers, execs.

Sell fast, well-structured briefs and a monthly update pack instead of one-off manual research logs.

What's After AutoResearch: Agent Hub

Karpathy also launched Agent Hub. Think of it like GitHub, but for agents. An agent swarm collaboration platform.

From the GitHub repo: "A bare Git repo, a message board designed for a swarm of agents working on the same codebase." No main branch. No PRs. No merges. A sprawling DAG of commits with a message board for agents to coordinate.

When Karpathy builds something, pay attention.

##### 1. Autoresearch 是什么：一个会 “自己做实验” 的 AI 循环系统
- Karpathy 推出了开源项目**autoresearch**，目前已有**25,000+ GitHub stars**，增长很快。
- 它的工作方式很简单：
  1. 你给它一个目标；
  2. AI 代理自己制定计划；
  3. 修改 Python 代码；
  4. 在 GPU 上跑一个短实验（**大约 5 分钟**）；
  5. 读取结果；
  6. 决定下一步怎么改；
  7. 反复循环。
- 你只需要告诉它 “什么叫更好”，比如：
  - 更低获客成本
  - 更多点击
  - 更高销售额
  - 更好的模型分数
- 它只保留带来提升的改动。  
**通俗理解：**像一个不会累的研究助理，一直帮你试错、筛选、保留最优版本。

---

##### 2. 它的核心思路：像一个 “你可以指挥的研究主管”
文章把它总结为一个很清晰的心智模型：

###### 第一步：定义清楚任务
- 任务要明确、可衡量。
- 例子：
  - 代码实验：**“提高这个模型的测试分数”**
  - 商业研究：**“找出 XYZ 产品的前五个竞争对手并写简短报告”**

###### 第二步：给它必要权限
- 做模型 / 代码实验：需要**代码 + GPU**
- 做资料研究：需要**互联网 + 文档访问能力**

###### 第三步：让它进入循环
- 它会不断执行：
  - 规划
  - 行动（跑代码 / 搜信息）
  - 读结果
  - 调整计划

###### 第四步：过段时间再回来查看
- 可以**6、12、20 小时后**回来。
- 系统会留下完整记录：
  - 图表
  - 指标
  - 通俗语言写的总结

##### 具体实例：用 autoresearch 做 “落地页标题自动优化”

###### 场景
你是一个卖 AI 简历优化服务的 SaaS 创业者，目标是提升落地页注册转化率。

###### 任务定义
给 autoresearch 一个清晰目标：

> 在现有落地页基础上，自动生成并测试不同标题、副标题和 CTA 文案，使注册转化率在 7 天内提升 15%，同时不提高获客成本。

###### 它如何工作
agent 循环执行：

1. 读取当前落地页文案与历史转化数据  
2. 生成新的标题 / 副标题 / 按钮文案组合  
3. 自动上线小流量 A/B 测试  
4. 读取 CTR、注册率、跳出率等指标  
5. 保留优胜版本，淘汰差版本  
6. 继续迭代下一轮

###### 结果输出
第二天你看到：

- 最优标题版本
- 各版本实验数据对比
- 哪类文案更有效的总结
- 推荐正式上线的页面版本

---

##### 基于该实例的最佳实践（精炼）

###### 1. 先把 “更好” 定义成单一核心 KPI
不要说 “帮我优化营销效果”，要说：

- 主目标：注册转化率提升
- 约束条件：CAC 不上升、跳出率不过高

**最佳实践：一次只优化一个核心指标。**

---

###### 2. 从 “小而可测” 的变量开始
优先让 agent 改：

- 标题
- 副标题
- CTA 按钮文案

不要一开始就同时改：

- 页面结构
- 配色
- 定价
- 受众定位

**最佳实践：先优化高影响、低复杂度变量。**

---

###### 3. 设计可自动评估的 bench
autoresearch 的关键不是 “会改”，而是 “能评估”。

你的 bench 脚本应自动返回：

- 展示量
- 点击率
- 注册转化率
- 每个版本的统计显著性

**最佳实践：没有自动评估，就没有高质量闭环。**

---

###### 4. 给 agent 明确边界
告诉它：

- 只能修改文案，不改价格
- 不得承诺虚假收益
- 不得改变品牌语气
- 单次实验流量上限 10%

**最佳实践：让 AI 在护栏内优化，而不是无限自由发挥。**

---

###### 5. 保留人工审批的 “accept” 环节
即使某版本数据更好，也不要直接全量上线。

应该：

- AI 提交候选优胜版本
- 人来审核是否合规、是否伤害品牌
- 再决定是否发布

**最佳实践：AI 负责搜索空间，人类负责最终决策。**

---

###### 6. 优先跑短周期、低成本实验
比如每轮先跑 2–4 小时的小流量测试，而不是一上来跑大预算广告。

**最佳实践：先用便宜实验筛选，再把预算给赢家。**

---

###### 7. 所有实验必须可回溯
保留：

- 改了什么
- 为什么改
- 数据结果如何
- 为什么被保留 / 放弃

**最佳实践：日志和版本记录与优化本身同等重要。**

---

###### 8. 先在熟悉的垂直场景落地
比如你已经懂：

- SaaS 注册页
- 电商商品页
- 招聘广告页

不要一开始跨到完全陌生领域。

**最佳实践：先在你已理解用户、指标、风险的业务里使用 autoresearch。**

---

##### 一句话总结
**autoresearch 最适合用在 “目标明确、反馈可量化、实验成本低、允许快速迭代” 的场景。**
