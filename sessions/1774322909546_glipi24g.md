## 正文

The Insights Agent automatically analyzes your traces to detect usage patterns, common agent behaviors and failure modes—without requiring you to manually review thousands of traces.Insights uses hierarchical categorization to make sense of your data and highlight actionable trends.

Insights is available for LangSmith Plus and Enterprise [plans](https://www.langchain.com/pricing) and is only available for LangSmith SaaS deployments.

#### Prerequisites

- A model configuration set up for Insights in your workspace.
- Permissions to create rules in LangSmith (required to generate new Insights Reports).
- Permissions to view tracing projects LangSmith (required to view existing Insights Reports).

#### Generate your first Insights report

![](https://mintcdn.com/langchain-5e9cc07a/rp5c1TvRWS7-YcPd/langsmith/images/insights-autogenerate-config.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=rp5c1TvRWS7-YcPd&q=85&s=1055fe5ac43cdce00c43297e818db6b6)

Auto configuration flow for Insights Agent

###### From the LangSmith UI

1. Navigate to **Tracing Projects** in the left-hand menu and select a tracing project.
2. Click **+New** in the top right corner then **New Insights Report** to generate new insights over the project.
3. Enter a name for your job.
4. If you haven’t already, configure a model for Insights in your workspace settings.
5. Answer the guided questions to focus your Insights Report on what you want to learn about your agent, then click **Run job**.

Toggle to Manual mode to configure the job manually.

This will kick off a background Insights Report. Reports can take up to 30 minutes to complete.

###### From the

You can generate Insights Reports over data stored outside LangSmith using the Python SDK. This allows you to analyze chat histories from your production systems, logs, or other sources.When you call `generate_insights()`, the SDK will:

1. Upload your chat histories as traces to a new LangSmith project
2. Generate an Insights Report over those uploaded traces
3. Return a link to your results in the LangSmith UI

Generating insights over 1,000 threads typically costs $1.00-$2.00 with OpenAI models and $3.00-$4.00 with current Anthropic models. The cost scales with the number of threads sampled and the size of each thread.

#### Understand the results

Once your job has completed, you can navigate to the **Insights** tab where you’ll see a table of Insights Report. Each Report contains insights generated over a specific sample of traces from the tracing project.![](https://mintcdn.com/langchain-5e9cc07a/lC4dVnXBXgUXGkP0/langsmith/images/insights-job-results.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=lC4dVnXBXgUXGkP0&q=85&s=1a95e426b2984bcf417f92d1c4d739b6)

Insights Reports for a single tracing project

Click into your job to see traces organized into a set of auto-generated categories.You can drill down through categories and subcategories to view the underlying traces, feedback, and run statistics.![](https://mintcdn.com/langchain-5e9cc07a/4-kFQm9_42J5OnwH/langsmith/images/insights-nav.gif?s=6a22bfd0d94262b7aa78468a8379ea0f)

Common topics of conversations with the [https://chat.langchain.com](https://chat.langchain.com) chatbot

##### Executive summary

At the top of each report, you’ll find an executive summary that surfaces the most important patterns discovered in your traces. This includes:

- Key findings with percentages showing how often each pattern appears.
- Clickable references (e.g., #1, #2, #3) to traces the agent identified as exceptionally relevant to your question.

![](https://mintcdn.com/langchain-5e9cc07a/Of41ZZ4fTt77Vjj5/langsmith/images/insights-summary.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=Of41ZZ4fTt77Vjj5&q=85&s=d565a549c83bad50398fa7a276eae0af)

Executive summary showing key patterns with trace references

##### Top-level categories

Your traces are automatically grouped into top-level categories that represent the broadest patterns in your data.The distribution bars show how frequently each pattern occurs, making it easy to spot behaviors that happen more or less than expected.Each category has a brief description and displays aggregated metrics over the traces it contains, including:

- Typical trace stats (like error rates, latency, cost)
- Feedback scores from your evaluators
- Attributes extracted as part of the job

##### Subcategories

Clicking on any category shows a breakdown into subcategories, which gives you a more granular understanding of interaction patterns in that category of traces.In the [Chat Langchain](https://chat.langchain.com) example pictured above, under “Data &amp; Retrieval” there are subcategories like “Vector Stores” and “Data Ingestion”.

##### Individual traces

You can view the traces assigned to each category or subcategory by clicking through to see the traces table. From there, you can click into any trace to see the full conversation details.

#### Configure a job

You can create an Insights Report using the auto-generated flow or by configuring it manually.

##### Autogenerating a config

1. Open **New Insights** and make sure the **Auto** toggle is active.
2. Answer the natural-language questions about your agent’s purpose, what you want to learn, and how traces are structured. Insights will translate your answers into a draft config (job name, summary prompt, attributes, and sampling defaults).
3. Choose a provider, then click **Generate config** to preview or **Run job** to launch immediately.

**Providing useful context** For best results, write a sentence or two for each prompt that gives the agent the context it needs—what you’re trying to learn, which signals or fields matter most, and anything you already know isn’t useful. The clearer you are about what your agent does and how its traces are structured, the more the Insights Agent can group examples in a way that’s specific, actionable, and aligned with how you reason about your data.**Describing your traces** Explain how your data is organized—are these single runs or multi-turn conversations? Which inputs and outputs contain the key information? This helps the Insights Agent generate summary prompts and attributes that focus on what matters. You can also directly specify variables from the summary prompt section if needed.

##### Choose models

Insights uses two models:

- **Thinking model** — performs the clustering step (more capable, higher cost).
- **Summarization model** — generates the per-trace summaries (faster, lower cost).

Both models are selected from the providers you have configured in your workspace. When specific models have been enabled for Insights in your model configurations, you can select them individually. If no individual models are configured, you select a provider (OpenAI or Anthropic) and Insights uses default models for that provider.For best results, use models from the same provider for both roles.

##### Manual configuration

Manual configuration gives you more control—for example, predefining categories you want your data grouped into or targeting traces that match specific feedback scores and filters.

###### Select traces

- **Sample size**: The maximum number of traces to analyze. Currently capped at 1,000
- **Time range**: Traces are sampled from this time range
- **Filters**: Additional trace filters. As you adjust filters, you’ll see how many traces match your criteria

By default, top-level categories are automatically generated bottom-up from the underlying traces.In some instances, you know specific categories you’re interested in upfront and want the job to bucket traces into those predefined categories.The **Categories** section of the config lets you do this by enumerating the names and descriptions of the top-level categories you want to be used.Subcategories are still auto-generated by the algorithm within the predefined top-level categories.When a job completes, the discovered top-level categories are automatically saved back to the config—but only if the config had no categories defined beforehand. This means subsequent scheduled runs will reuse those categories for consistency.

###### Summary prompt

The first step of the job is to create a brief summary of every trace—it is these summaries that are then categorized.Extracting the right information in the summary is essential for getting useful categories.The prompt used to generate these summaries can be edited.The two things to think about when editing the prompt are:

- Summarization instructions: Any information that isn’t in the trace summary won’t affect the categories that get generated, so make sure to provide clear instructions on what information is important to extract from each trace.
- Trace content: Use mustache formatting to specify which parts of each trace are passed to the summarizer. Large traces with lots of inputs and outputs can be expensive and noisy. Reducing the prompt to only include the most relevant parts of the trace can improve your results.

You must specify what parts of each trace to send to the summarizer using at least one of these template variables:You can access nested fields using dot notation. For example, `{{run.inputs.foo.bar}}` includes only the `bar` field within `foo` in the last run’s inputs.

For projects with threads, Insights analyzes full conversations. Only the most recent root run from each thread is used for `run.*` variables. Use `all_thread_messages` to access the complete conversation history.

###### Attributes

Along with a summary, you can define additional string, numerical, and boolean attributes to be extracted from each trace. These attributes will influence the categorization step—traces with similar attribute values will tend to be categorized together. You can also see aggregations of these attributes per category.As an example, you might want to extract the attribute `user_satisfied: boolean` from each trace to steer the algorithm towards categories that split up positive and negative user experiences, and to see the average user satisfaction per category.

###### Filter attributes

You can use the `filter_by` parameter on boolean attributes to pre-filter traces before generating insights. When enabled, only traces where the attribute evaluates to `true` are included in the analysis.This is useful when you want to focus your Insights Report on a specific subset of traces—for example, only analyzing errors, only examining English-language conversations, or only including traces that meet certain quality criteria.![](https://mintcdn.com/langchain-5e9cc07a/L4LVgASBXoDKblmJ/langsmith/images/insights-filter-by-attribute.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=L4LVgASBXoDKblmJ&q=85&s=8cb30778befb18af445c3f6db758e631)

Using filter attributes to generate Insights only on traces with agent errors

**How it works:**

- Add `"filter_by": true` to any boolean attribute when creating a config for the Insights Agent
- The LLM evaluates each trace against the attribute description during summarization
- Traces where the attribute is `false` or missing are excluded before insights are generated

#### Schedule Insights Reports

Schedule Insights reports to run automatically on a recurring basis. When creating or editing a configuration, use the **Schedule** section to choose:

- **Daily** - Runs every day at 8:00 UTC
- **Weekly on Monday** - Runs every Monday at 8:00 UTC
- **Custom** - Enter your own cron expression (in UTC)

Each scheduled run generates a new report using your saved configuration. Time ranges are computed dynamically—for example, “last 24 hours” always analyzes the most recent 24-hour window at execution time.

#### Save your config

You can optionally save configs for future reuse using the ‘save as’ button. This is especially useful if you want to compare Insights Reports over time to identify changes in user and agent behavior.Select from previously saved configs in the dropdown in the top-left corner of the pane when creating a new Insights Report.

#### 主旨概括

这篇文章介绍了 LangSmith 的`Insights Agent`：它可以自动分析大量 trace，帮助用户快速发现使用模式、常见行为和失败原因，不必手动翻看成千上万条记录。文章重点说明了它的适用条件、生成报告的方法、结果如何解读，以及如何通过配置提升分析效果。

##### 1. Insights Agent 是什么、适用于谁

- `Insights Agent`的核心作用是：自动分析 trace，并用 “分层分类” 的方式，把杂乱数据整理成可执行的规律和趋势。
- 它能帮助用户发现：
  - 常见使用模式
  - Agent 的典型行为
  - 常见失败模式
- 适用范围有限：
  - 仅支持`LangSmith Plus`和`Enterprise`套餐
  - 仅可用于`LangSmith SaaS`部署
- 使用前需要满足两个前提：
  - 工作区里已经配置好可供 Insights 使用的模型
  - 拥有相应权限：
    - 创建规则的权限（用于生成新的 Insights Report）
    - 查看 tracing project 的权限（用于查看已有报告）

##### 2. 如何生成第一份 Insights 报告

###### 2.1 在 LangSmith UI 中生成

- 操作路径比较直接：
  - 进入左侧菜单中的`Tracing Projects`
  - 选择一个 tracing project
  - 点击右上角`+New` → `New Insights Report`
  - 输入任务名称
  - 如果还没配置模型，需要先在 workspace settings 中配置
  - 回答引导问题，说明你想了解 agent 的哪些情况
  - 点击`Run job`
- 如果不想用自动引导，也可以切换到`Manual mode`手动配置。
- 报告是在后台运行的，完成时间最长可达`30 分钟`。

###### 2.2 通过 Python SDK 生成

- 如果数据不在 LangSmith 里，也可以用 Python SDK 分析外部数据，比如：
  - 生产环境聊天记录
  - 系统日志
  - 其他来源的数据
- 调用`generate_insights ()`后，SDK 会自动完成三件事：
  - 把聊天历史上传为新的 trace，存到 LangSmith 项目里
  - 基于这些 trace 生成 Insights Report
  - 返回一个 LangSmith UI 链接，方便查看结果
- 成本示例：
  - 分析`1,000`个线程，使用 OpenAI 模型通常花费`1.00–2.00 美元`
  - 使用当前 Anthropic 模型通常花费`3.00–4.00 美元`
- 成本会随着两个因素增长：
  - 抽样线程数量
  - 每个线程内容的大小

##### 3. 报告生成后，如何理解结果

###### 3.1 报告整体结构

- 任务完成后，可以在`Insights`标签页看到一个报告表格。
- 每份报告都对应 tracing project 中某一批被抽样的 trace。
- 点进某个报告后，trace 会按自动生成的类别组织起来，支持逐层下钻查看。

###### 3.2 Executive summary（执行摘要）

- 每份报告顶部都有执行摘要，用于快速告诉你最重要的发现。
- 它通常包括：
  - 关键发现
  - 每种模式出现的百分比
  - 可点击的 trace 引用，例如`#1`、`#2`、`#3`
- 这些引用会指向系统认为 “特别相关” 的具体 trace，方便用户快速验证。

###### 3.3 Top-level categories（顶层分类）

- 所有 trace 会被自动归入若干顶层类别，这些类别代表数据中最主要的模式。
- 界面中的分布条会显示每种模式出现的频率，方便判断哪些行为 “比预期更常见” 或 “更少见”。
- 每个分类还会附带聚合指标，例如：
  - 典型 trace 指标：错误率、延迟、成本
  - 来自 evaluator 的反馈分数
  - 任务中提取出的属性

###### 3.4 Subcategories（子分类）

- 点击顶层分类后，还可以看到更细的子分类，帮助理解同一类交互下的具体差异。
- 文中例子里，在 Chat LangChain 的`Data & Retrieval`类别下，会继续细分出：
  - `Vector Stores`
  - `Data Ingestion`

###### 3.5 Individual traces（单条 trace）

- 用户可以继续点击进入某个分类或子分类对应的 trace 列表。
- 再点开任意一条 trace，就能查看完整的对话和运行细节。

##### 4. 如何配置一个 Insights 任务

文章强调：可以使用自动生成配置，也可以手动配置；两种方式适合不同场景。

###### 4.1 自动生成配置

- 在`New Insights`页面中保持`Auto`开关开启。
- 用户用自然语言回答几个问题，例如：
  - 你的 agent 是做什么的
  - 你想从数据中学到什么
  - trace 是如何组织的
- 系统会把回答转换成一份草稿配置，包括：
  - job name
  - summary prompt
  - attributes
  - sampling defaults
- 然后选择模型提供商，点击：
  - `Generate config`可预览配置
  - `Run job`可直接开始任务

###### 4.2 提供清晰上下文的重要性

- 文档特别提醒：为了得到更有用的结果，最好对每个问题写`1–2 句`明确说明背景。
- 重点要交代清楚：
  - 你真正想了解什么
  - 哪些信号或字段最重要
  - 哪些信息你已经知道 “不重要”
- 原因是：你描述得越清楚，Insights Agent 就越能按 “符合你业务理解” 的方式来分组，而不是只给出泛泛而谈的类别。

###### 4.3 如何描述 trace 结构

- 需要说明数据组织方式，例如：
  - 是单次运行，还是多轮对话
  - 哪些输入输出字段最关键
- 这样系统才能生成更精准的总结 prompt 和提取属性。
- 如果需要，也可以直接在 summary prompt 中指定变量。

##### 5. 模型如何选择

- Insights 依赖两类模型：
  - `Thinking model`：负责聚类，能力更强，但成本更高
  - `Summarization model`：负责生成每条 trace 的摘要，速度更快、成本更低
- 这两个模型都来自工作区中已配置的 provider。
- 如果工作区已经给 Insights 单独启用了具体模型，可以直接单独选模型。
- 如果没有单独配置，就只能选择 provider（如`OpenAI`或`Anthropic`），系统会自动使用默认模型。
- 官方建议：两个角色最好使用同一 provider 的模型，效果通常更稳定。

##### 6. 手动配置：适合更精细的分析

###### 6.1 控制抽样范围

手动模式下可以精确控制要分析哪些 trace：

- `Sample size`：最多分析多少条 trace
  - 当前上限是`1,000`
- `Time range`：从哪个时间范围抽样
- `Filters`：附加筛选条件
  - 调整时界面会实时显示有多少条 trace 符合条件

###### 6.2 预定义顶层分类

- 默认情况下，顶层分类是系统根据 trace 自下而上自动生成的。
- 但某些场景下，用户一开始就知道自己关注哪些类别。
- 这时可以在`Categories`部分手动列出顶层分类的名称和描述，让系统按这些类别归桶。
- 即使顶层分类预设好了，子分类仍然会由算法自动生成。
- 还有一个细节：
  - 如果配置里原本没有定义分类，那么任务完成后，系统发现的顶层分类会自动保存回配置
  - 这样后续定时任务就能复用同一套分类，保证长期比较时更一致

##### 7. Summary prompt 很关键：它决定分类质量

- 任务的第一步，是先为每条 trace 生成简短摘要。
- 后续分类其实是基于这些摘要来做的。
- 所以，摘要提取得是否准确，直接决定分类是否有价值。

###### 7.1 编辑 summary prompt 时要关注两点

- `Summarization instructions`：
  - 如果某些关键信息没有出现在摘要里，它就不会影响后续分类
  - 所以要明确写出：每条 trace 应该提取哪些重点
- `Trace content`：
  - 需要用 mustache 模板指定，把 trace 的哪些部分传给 summarizer
  - 如果 trace 很大、输入输出很多，既会增加成本，也会引入噪音
  - 因此只保留最相关的字段，通常能得到更好的结果

###### 7.2 模板变量的使用

- 必须至少指定一个模板变量，把 trace 内容传给 summarizer。
- 支持使用点号访问嵌套字段，例如：
  - `{{run.inputs.foo.bar}}`
  - 表示只取最后一次 run 输入中的`foo.bar`
- 如果项目有 thread，Insights 会分析完整对话。
- 但对于`run.*`变量，只会使用每个 thread 中 “最近的根 run”。
- 如果要访问完整会话历史，需要用`all_thread_messages`。

##### 8. Attributes：让分类更贴近业务目标

- 除了摘要，还可以为每条 trace 提取额外属性，支持：
  - 字符串
  - 数值
  - 布尔值
- 这些属性会影响分类过程：
  - 属性值相近的 trace，更容易被分到一起
- 系统也会在每个类别里展示这些属性的聚合结果。

###### 8.1 文中举的例子

- 可以提取一个布尔属性：
  - `user_satisfied: boolean`
- 这样算法就更容易把 “用户满意” 和 “用户不满意” 的案例区分开。
- 同时，用户还可以在每个类别里看到平均满意度，帮助判断问题集中在哪些交互模式里。

##### 9. Filter attributes：只分析你关心的那部分数据

- 可以在布尔属性上设置`filter_by`参数，作为预过滤条件。
- 开启后，只有该属性评估为`true`的 trace 才会进入分析。
- 这特别适合做聚焦分析，比如：
  - 只分析报错案例
  - 只分析英文对话
  - 只分析满足某种质量标准的记录

###### 9.1 它的工作方式

- 在某个布尔属性配置里添加：
  - `"filter_by": true`
- 然后系统会在摘要阶段由 LLM 判断每条 trace 是否符合该属性描述。
- 如果属性结果为`false`或缺失，该 trace 就会在生成 insights 前被排除。

###### 9.2 具体例子

- 文中提到：可以利用 filter attributes，只对 “agent 出错” 的 trace 生成 Insights。
- 这样报告会更聚焦于失败模式，而不是被正常案例稀释。

##### 10. 支持定时生成报告

- Insights Reports 可以定时自动运行。
- 在创建或编辑配置时，可以在`Schedule`部分设置：
  - `Daily`：每天`8:00 UTC`
  - `Weekly on Monday`：每周一`8:00 UTC`
  - `Custom`：自定义 cron 表达式（UTC）
- 每次定时运行都会基于保存好的配置生成一份新报告。
- 时间范围是动态计算的，比如：
  - “最近 24 小时” 在每次执行时，都会重新取最新的 24 小时数据

##### 11. 保存配置，方便长期对比

- 用户可以用`save as`保存配置，供以后复用。
- 这对长期追踪很有帮助，因为可以持续比较不同时间段的报告，观察：
  - 用户行为变化
  - agent 行为变化
- 创建新报告时，也可以在左上角下拉框中选择以前保存过的配置，避免重复设置。

#### 总结

这篇文章的重点是：`Insights Agent`通过自动摘要、分类、属性提取和过滤机制，把原本难以人工消化的大量 trace 转化成结构化洞察。它不仅适合一次性排查问题，也很适合通过保存配置和定时运行，持续监控 agent 的表现变化。
