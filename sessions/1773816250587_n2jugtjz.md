#### How to Solve Claude AI Account Suspension: "Your account has been disabled after an automatic review of your recent activities."

Users have reported that Claude AI accounts are frequently suspended, leading to many people having their accounts disabled. This article explains the reasons for account suspension on Claude AI, guides you on how to avoid this issue, and how to continue using Claude AI after your account has been disabled.

![Your account has been disabled after an automatic review of your recent activities](https://hub-apac-1.lobeobjects.space/blog/assets/48d940c120daf2eb732eee380fdeac5d.webp)

Recently, many Claude AI users have reported being disabled when trying to log in and receiving the error message, “Your account has been disabled after an automatic review of your recent activities.”

This message indicates that Claude AI's automatic review system has detected unusual activity on your account and has temporarily disabled your access, essentially meaning your account has been suspended. This action is a protective measure aimed at preventing abuse and improper use.

##### Types of Claude Account Suspensions

Here are some common suspension error messages:

1. **Your account has been disabled after an automatic review of your recent activities.**

- This message indicates that your account has been disabled due to detected unusual activity by the automatic review. Users should review the terms of service and acceptable use policy for more information.

1. **account\_banned**

- This is a direct suspension message indicating that the user can no longer use the account.

1. **Invalid authorization**

- This error typically indicates that the user's login credentials are invalid, which could be due to the account being disabled or other security issues.

---

#### Why is my Claude AI Account Disabled?

Currently, Claude AI's risk control policies regarding accounts are very strict. Please refer to the official [Terms of Service](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/consumer-terms) and [Usage Policy](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/aup) for specific details.

The main reasons for account suspension include:

- **Frequent IP address changes**: Using a proxy server or VPN causes frequent IP address changes. The system may view this as suspicious activity, leading to account suspension.
- **Using unsupported registration methods**: For instance, accounts registered using temporary phone number services are often directly banned.
- **Geographical restrictions**: Certain regions may not have access to Claude for policy reasons, which can also lead to account suspension.
- **Violating terms of service**: Actions such as posting inappropriate content, abusing the API, or engaging in malicious activities like fraudulent transactions can lead to account suspension.

---

#### How to Lift the Suspension After an Account is Disabled?

If you are certain you have not violated Claude AI's usage terms, you can try to appeal the suspension manually. You will need to fill out an appeal form or send an email directly to usersafety@anthropic.com with detailed account information. For more details, refer to the [official instructions](https://support.anthropic.com/en/articles/8241253-trust-and-safety-warnings-and-appeals).

Please note that the chances of recovering an account suspended in the earlier days were higher through official channels, but recently Claude has tightened risk controls. If you cannot provide sufficient evidence to verify your account's legitimacy, you may not be able to recover the account, regardless of whether you are a PRO member; you could permanently lose access.

##### Email Template

Based on the following email template, include your account information, your last login location, and if necessary, clarify potential reasons for the account suspension to increase your chances of recovery.

```
Dear anthropic,

While I acknowledge that account suspensions may be necessary in certain circumstances, such as security breaches or violations of the terms and conditions, I have not received any information indicating that such issues have occurred in my case. Thank you for your attention to this matter. I anticipate a prompt and satisfactory response from your team.
```

#### How to Prevent Your Claude AI Account from Being Suspended?

To avoid account suspension, it is recommended to:

##### Use a Stable Network Environment

- Do not use Claude AI in unsupported regions.
- Use a clean and stable network environment, and avoid frequent IP address changes.

##### Maintain Proper Usage Behavior

- Strictly adhere to platform rules and terms of use.
- Use accurate and valid registration information.
- Control access frequency, avoid making large-scale requests in a short time, and refrain from logging in from multiple devices simultaneously.
