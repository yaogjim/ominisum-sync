## 正文

⨯ Confused by Linux / SteamOS Gaming? Be sure to [check out our growing guides section](https://www.gamingonlinux.com/guides/).

While Valve will *not* be making and providing their own e-ink display for the Steam Machine, they have opened it up so anyone can now do it. Valve originally teased it with the first lot of reviewers that got their hands on it.

All of it is available on their [GitLab](https://gitlab.steamos.cloud/SteamHardware/SteamMachine/inkterface) under the MIT license, which goes over everything you need to make your own and stick it on the front of your fancy new Steam Machine.

![Steam Machine e-ink display](https://uploads.golmedia.net/uploads/articles/article_media/15354839511783064248gol2.webp)  
*Image Credit - [Gamers Nexus](https://gamersnexus.net/pre-built-pc/valve-steam-machine-review-gpu-cpu-benchmarks-steamos-test-thermals-noise-and-price)*

They're now calling it the "Inkterface" and there's a good few things you'll need to make it including:

> - 1 x Adafruit ESP32 Feather with 2MB PSRAM.
> - 1 x Adafruit eInk Breakout Friend.
> - 1 x Adafruit 5.83" Monochrome eInk Panel.
> - 13 x M2.5 x 5mm Pan Head Machine Screws.
> - 4 x 1/4" x 1/4" x 3/16" Stepped Magnet SB443-OUT.

Valve even provided a video on the GitLab showing it being put together, which we're re-hosting to make it easily viewable for you:

Pretty cool to see.

Maybe we will see some other vendors actually do them pre-built for us. [JSAUX teased they would be doing it](https://www.gamingonlinux.com/2025/11/jsaux-are-teasing-steam-machine-front-panels-with-built-in-screens/) back in November 2025, and checking back today they've [said](https://x.com/jsauxofficial/status/2072934757672034665) they still plan to do "Ink &amp; Pixel versions". If the Steam Machine is popular enough - no doubt we'll have other accessory brands do various versions of their own.

#### Valve 开源 Steam Machine 墨水屏方案，第三方配件生态有望形成

##### 核心内容

Valve 不会亲自生产 Steam Machine 的电子墨水屏配件，但已将整套方案以 **“Inkterface”** 命名并开源。用户和厂商可以自行制作，将其安装在 Steam Machine 正面，用于显示信息或个性化内容。

项目代码、制作说明和组装视频均已发布在 Valve 的 GitLab 上，并采用宽松的 **MIT 许可证**，支持个人改造与商业化使用。

##### 制作所需硬件

主要组件包括：

- Adafruit ESP32 Feather 开发板
- Adafruit eInk Breakout Friend 驱动板
- 5.83 英寸黑白电子墨水屏
- 13 颗 M2.5 螺丝
- 4 块阶梯式磁铁

##### 产品与商业价值

Valve 选择“开源方案而非官方量产”，可以降低自身硬件运营成本，同时鼓励玩家、创客和配件厂商共同扩展 Steam Machine 生态。

配件厂商 **JSAUX** 已确认仍计划推出 **Ink 与 Pixel 两种版本**。如果 Steam Machine 获得足够市场规模，预计会有更多品牌推出预制成品和差异化设计。

##### 一句话总结

Valve 正通过开放硬件方案，把 Steam Machine 的个性化配件交给社区和第三方厂商，尝试复制 Steam Deck 周边生态的增长路径。
