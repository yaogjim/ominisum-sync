**Chris**

### how to make your first $1 with AI

if you haven't made your first dollar online yet, this is for you, i'm talking to the person who's been consuming content for months, maybe years, and still hasn't figured out what to actually sell

i'm gonna tell you exactly what i did

i took something i personally dealt with for years, used AI to turn my knowledge into a simple PDF guide, and sold it to people going through the same thing. that was it

you already know something worth selling. i promise you do. maybe you dealt with a health issue and figured out how to manage it. maybe you taught yourself a skill, whatever and came out the other side knowing things most people don't

if you spent real time on something, there's someone at step 1 right now who would pay to skip ahead

so take that knowledge, all of it, what worked, what didn't, the stuff you wish someone told you from the start, and feed it to AI. tell it to structure it into a guide. you'll have a clean PDF in a few minutes

that's literally what i did. i gave manus my full story and everything i learned, told it to turn it into a proper guide. used claude to help me figure out how to package it. then i asked manus to give me a prompt to build a landing page in lovable and it did that too

make a simple landing page for it. doesn't need to look amazing. just needs to explain the problem and show them you have the answer, you can use AI website builders for this

now the part that actually matters, distribution.

i went straight to the subreddits where people with my exact problem hang out every day and started posting things that genuinely helped people

i used claude to write the posts. i'd give it the topic and my perspective and it would draft the post for me. but here's the thing, you can't just copy paste what AI gives you. people recognise AI writing now. so i'd always rewrite it in my own voice, make it sound like me

not commenting on other people's stuff. i was making my own posts. sharing what actually worked for me, tips people could use right away and it worked pretty much from day one.

never linked my product, just showed up and was providing value

people started dming me almost instantly. someone would dm me telling me what they were going through, asking how to deal with it. so i'd actually help them. back and forth, sometimes 10-15 messages deep, just real advice for their situation based on what worked for me. only after that, when it naturally came up, i'd mention the guide. by that point they already knew i knew my stuff so it never felt like a pitch. some people took the free advice and never bought anything. and honestly that's fine. i was helping either way and that was the whole point

got to $300 MRR in a couple weeks. turned the PDF into a monthly newsletter so people kept getting value and i kept getting paid

reason i went subscription is because the niche was small. fewer total buyers means you need to make more per person over time. a one-time PDF has a ceiling. a monthly newsletter where people keep getting ongoing value doesn't. and at $9/month it's a no-brainer for someone who's actively struggling with something. low enough that nobody has to think twice

i still rank #1 on google for some of those searches, free traffic every day

and if you're reading this thinking "i don't have anything worth selling" — you do. you're just too close to it to see it. the thing you take for granted because you've lived with it for years is the exact thing someone else is desperately searching for answers on right now.

so if you're stuck trying to figure out what to sell, stop looking outward. look at what you already know. look at what you've already been through. package it, post where these people already hang out, be genuinely helpful, and the money follows

one more thing, if you're using AI to help you write, make sure it sounds like you. people catch on fast. use it to get your ideas out but always run it through your own voice.

## 解读（1）

### 2026-03-17 07:06:09

##### 一句话总结

这篇内容的核心是：**先卖你已经掌握的经验，用 AI 快速包装成产品，再去目标人群聚集的地方持续提供价值，通过真实交流完成转化。**

##### 深入浅出地解读

###### 1. 他卖的不是“AI”，而是“解决方案”
作者并不是靠 AI 本身赚钱，而是把自己多年解决某个问题的经验，整理成了一个 PDF 指南，再卖给有同样困扰的人。

通俗说：  
**AI 只是包装工具，真正值钱的是你知道什么、你解决过什么。**

---

###### 2. 适合普通人起步的方法：卖“自己走过的路”
文章强调一个很重要的点：  
很多人总觉得自己“没东西可卖”，其实不是没有，而是**太熟悉了，所以感觉不值钱**。

比如你可能：
- 自学过一项技能
- 解决过某个健康、效率、学习或工作问题
- 踩过很多坑，最后找到有效方法

这些经验，对你来说是“日常”，对别人来说可能是“捷径”。

---

###### 3. AI 提高的是“出产品速度”
作者的做法很简单：
- 用 AI 整理经验，生成 PDF 指南
- 用 AI 帮忙想包装方式
- 用 AI 生成落地页文案或建站提示词
- 用 AI 辅助写社区内容

这说明 AI 最适合做的是：
**整理、提炼、包装、提速**，而不是凭空创造价值。

---

###### 4. 真正决定能不能赚到钱的，是“分发”
作者特别强调：  
产品做好以后，最重要的不是继续打磨，而是去**用户已经在的地方**出现，比如相关 Reddit 社区。

他的关键动作不是硬卖，而是：
- 发有帮助的内容
- 用自己的口吻改写 AI 草稿
- 先认真回复私信
- 在合适的时候自然提到产品

本质上就是一句话：  
**先建立信任，再发生购买。**

---

###### 5. 从一次性产品，变成订阅产品
他后来把 PDF 升级成月度 newsletter，原因也很现实：
- 细分市场小，买家总量有限
- 一次性收费有天花板
- 订阅能提高单个用户长期价值

这说明他的思路已经不只是“卖一份资料”，而是做成**持续输出价值的小生意**。

---

##### 我的意见

###### 我认同的地方
- **方向是对的**：从自己熟悉的问题切入，确实是普通人最容易起步的方式。
- **AI 的定位很清楚**：不是替你赚钱，而是帮你更快把经验变成产品。
- **分发思路靠谱**：去垂直社区持续提供帮助，比一上来投广告更适合新手。

###### 需要警惕的地方
- **不是任何经验都能卖**：你解决的问题，必须有人正在为它焦虑，最好还是愿意付费的那种。
- **不能只靠 AI 生成内容**：如果内容太“AI 味”，很容易失去信任。
- **某些领域有风险**：健康、法律、财务这类内容，如果没有专业资质，容易踩红线。

###### 更稳妥的做法
如果你想照着做，我建议按这个顺序：
1. 先找一个你真正解决过的问题
2. 去看目标人群是否真的在社区里反复提问
3. 先免费输出几篇有价值内容，验证需求
4. 再做最小产品，比如 PDF、清单、模板
5. 有人愿意买，再考虑做订阅或升级服务

##### 结论

这篇内容最有价值的地方，不是“怎么用 AI”，而是提醒你：

> **赚钱的起点，往往不是去追热点，而是把你已经知道的东西，变成别人愿意付费的答案。**
