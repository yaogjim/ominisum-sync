## 正文

![Photo illustration of a businessman turning away with his arms up in a gesture of defensiveness.](https://futurism.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/ai-companies-hide-debt-off-balance-sheet.jpg?quality=85&w=1152)

Illustration by Tag Hartman-Simkins / Futurism. Source: Shutterstock

Can’t-miss innovations from the bleeding edge of science and tech

AI companies are pouring untold billions of dollars into enormous data centers in their efforts to sustain increasingly complex and resource-intensive AI models.

It’s an extremely costly undertaking built on seemingly bottomless hype — and a mountain of debt. As Japanese financial newspaper [*Nikkei* *Asia* found in a recent investigation](https://asia.nikkei.com/business/technology/five-us-tech-giants-hidden-debts-soar-to-1.65tn-on-opaque-ai-funding), just five US tech giants — Alphabet, Microsoft, Amazon, Meta, and Oracle — are hiding an estimated $1.65 trillion in debt that doesn’t appear on balance sheets. That’s even more than the $1.35 trillion in debt the five companies officially reported in their financial data for the most recent quarter.

Meta alone has amassed around $420 billion in off-balance-sheet debt, according to *Nikkei*, highlighting how precarious the AI industry’s steep investment in AI has become, and [inspiring comparisons to energy company Enron](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/ai-sector-enron-sector-111500396.html), which collapsed in spectacular fashion in 2001 because of similar debts hidden behind shell companies. Like Enron, they’re using special purpose vehicles, or off-balance sheet arrangements such as legally distinct subsidiaries, as a way to make their financial reporting look healthier than it actually is — often a glaring sign that something is deeply amiss behind the scenes.

“The accounting treatment itself is in fashion,” technical accounting consultant Tom Selling [told *Bloomberg*](https://news.bloombergtax.com/financial-accounting/big-tech-ai-spree-revives-accounting-devices-that-toppled-enron). “But what if one of these companies was a house of cards and was propping itself up with this accounting treatment? To me, that’s the risk.”

Experts continue to [warn of an AI bubble](https://futurism.com/artificial-intelligence/problem-ai-industry-collapse-efficiency), noting the enormous and widening gulf between company valuations and their comparatively measly profits. The latest news will do little to quiet critics who say the situation is more dire than the companies’ official balance sheets suggest.

To keep up with the ongoing AI race, tech giants are committing vast sums to build out large-scale data center projects, a long-term bet that may — or may not — pay off. They’re also selling new shares to raise new funds, as *Nikkei* reports, which could lead to equity dilution and a drop in investor confidence.

That could make them even more vulnerable if the AI bubble does pop, or the industry fails to generate enough demand to justify the data center construction frenzy.

The pressure is on: four of the five companies *Nikkei* analyzed are set to report second quarter earnings in the coming days and weeks. We’ll be watching.

## 通俗精炼（1）

### 2026-07-29 01:20:25

#### AI巨头正在隐藏巨额债务

##### 核心结论

AI公司为建设大型数据中心、训练更复杂的模型，正在投入数千亿美元，并通过“表外融资”等方式降低财务报表上的债务规模。

据《日经亚洲》调查，Alphabet、微软、亚马逊、Meta和甲骨文五家公司合计约有 **1.65万亿美元表外债务**，高于其公开披露的 **1.35万亿美元债务**。其中，Meta的表外债务估计约为 **4200亿美元**。

##### 主要做法：将债务放到表外

这些公司可能通过特殊目的实体、独立子公司等结构持有数据中心和相关债务。

这样做可以：

- 减少资产负债表上的直接负债；
- 让财务状况看起来更健康；
- 将部分投资和风险转移到关联实体或合作方；
- 支撑公司继续大规模扩张AI基础设施。

但这并不意味着风险消失，只是从公司主体的报表中“隐藏”或延后体现。

##### 为什么风险正在上升

###### 1. AI基础设施投入巨大

建设数据中心、采购芯片和电力，需要长期、持续且规模庞大的资本支出。企业必须押注未来几年AI需求会快速增长，才能收回这些投资。

###### 2. AI收入增长未必匹配估值

当前AI公司的市场估值和基础设施投入不断上升，但实际利润和现金流增长相对有限，形成了较大的“估值—盈利”缺口。

###### 3. 融资压力可能加剧

部分公司通过发行新股筹集资金，这可能导致：

- 原有股东权益被稀释；
- 投资者信心下降；
- 公司对资本市场和债务融资更加依赖。

如果AI需求不足，数据中心产能过剩，公司可能难以偿还相关债务。

##### 与安然事件的类比

文章将这种做法与2001年倒闭的能源公司安然进行比较。安然曾通过特殊目的实体隐藏大量债务，最终因真实财务状况暴露而崩溃。

不过，使用表外结构本身并不一定违法或不合理。真正的风险在于：

> 如果公司依靠复杂的会计和融资安排，掩盖了自身无法持续盈利或偿债的问题，就可能形成“债务堆积但风险不透明”的脆弱结构。

##### 后续需要关注

- AI数据中心投资能否带来足够收入；
- 表外债务是否最终回到母公司资产负债表；
- 公司现金流和利息偿付能力；
- 新股发行是否导致明显股权稀释；
- AI行业是否存在估值泡沫和基础设施过剩。

##### 一句话总结

AI巨头正在用巨额资本建设未来，但部分债务被放在表外，使真实财务风险不够透明；如果AI需求和盈利增长跟不上基础设施投资，这些隐藏债务可能成为行业泡沫破裂的重要触发点。
